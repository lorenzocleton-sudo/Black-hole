Light paths in the equatorial plane

captured escapes b = 3√3/2 rs

Parallel rays from the right, spaced by impact parameter b. Any ray with b < 2.598 rs falls in, which is why the black "shadow" is 2.6× larger than the event horizon itself. Rings: horizon (rs), photon sphere (1.5 rs), ISCO (3 rs).

What you are seeing

Every pixel is a light ray traced backwards along a null geodesic of the Schwarzschild metric. The disk's far side appears bent over the top and under the bottom of the hole; the thin bright ring hugging the shadow is light that orbited the photon sphere. The side of the disk moving towards you is brighter and bluer (Doppler beaming ∝ g⁴), and light climbing out of the gravity well is redshifted.
